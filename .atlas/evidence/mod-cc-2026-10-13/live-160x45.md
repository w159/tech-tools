## Defects, exact text
1. Double border: pane is `╭─…✕─╮` wrapping a second `╭─…╮` box on every tab at both sizes.
2. Input label `▸ to lead ▏: type a message, Enter sends` has `▏:`.
3. The mod does not see Agent-tool workers. Worker names in `/tmp/atlas-live/.atlas/.run/channels.json` were `explorer-b620`, `explorer-efe9` (not `probe-explorer`/`probe-slow`) and the board has `explorer-*.jsonl`, yet band = idle, team = `nobody dispatched yet`, channel = `0 nicks`. Workers were Backgrounded; `← 3 agents` in the CC footer and the `⏺ main / ◯ atlas:explorer  slow scan  21s` rows are CC-native only.
4. 100x30 header wrap breaks the pipes: `ATLAS| 🏁     | probe-slow returned…`, continuation `        done     with the caveat…`, and `ATLAS| 🔍        |DISPATCH -> …` / `        research   /private…`. The space after ATLAS is lost (ANSI: ` ATLAS\x1b[0m|`), the glyph is cut and the phase word is dropped to the next line; `🏁     |probe-slow` has no space after the pipe.
5. Channel tab on 160 col: a stray `───────────────` row appears under the prompt rule (render artifact).
6. Prompt submit: `Ctrl+U` only removes one wrapped line at a time (needed several). Focus stayed in the mod after `Escape`; `Ctrl+X x` freed it. The earlier typed prompt was never submitted.
7. Worker lifetime: the probe-explorer worker finished in ~7s, so the band/team had no running window. A 38s `probe-slow` worker was used for the running-state captures.
