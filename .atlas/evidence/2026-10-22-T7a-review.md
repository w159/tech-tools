### xterm answer
isTypingIn omits the `.xterm` check that shortcuts.js:21-28 has. Practical risk is low: xterm.js captures keyboard through a hidden `textarea.xterm-helper-textarea`, so when the terminal is focused document.activeElement is a TEXTAREA and isTypingIn returns true. The gap is only if the wiring passes ev.target of a click on the canvas or a focused ancestor. This is a Minor item for the wiring unit: pass `() => isTypingIn(document.activeElement) || !!document.activeElement?.closest?.(".xterm")`, and confirm in the browser pass that `f`/`h`/`1` do nothing while the Agents terminal is focused.
